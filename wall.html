01 The wall

Every piece here is sealed.

Finished work, sealed and public. Open any piece to see its certificate: which account sealed the file, and when. Anyone can check one, no account needed.

Seal your own → How the seal works

    Certificate

    Your file is fingerprinted in this browser. It isn't uploaded.

    What this shows: the account named above sealed this exact file at the time shown. Change one pixel and the fingerprint stops matching.

    What it can't show: who made the art, whether it's original, or who owns it. The credit is whatever that account typed in. A seal is a receipt, not a judgement.
