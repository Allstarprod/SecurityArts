02 The seal

Every piece ends
in a receipt.

A postmark records when a letter went through. Ours does that for a file: we fingerprint the exact bytes, sign that fingerprint, and publish a certificate anyone can check. It doesn't judge the art — it records which account sealed which file, and when.

  • Checkable Anyone can verify the certificate — no account needed.
  • Honest It records who sealed which file, and when. It never claims more.
  • Exact Change one pixel and the fingerprint no longer matches.
