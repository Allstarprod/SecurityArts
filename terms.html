01What SecurityArts is

SecurityArts lets artists seal finished files and publish a public certificate for each one. It also shows sealed work on a public wall, lets anyone check a file against a certificate, and runs a marketplace where artists can sell and license work and take commissions. The security studio's services (penetration testing, reviews and similar work) are covered by a separate written agreement for each engagement, not by these terms.

02What a seal shows, and what it doesn't

When you seal a file, we calculate a SHA-256 fingerprint of its exact bytes and sign that fingerprint with the SecurityArts key (ECDSA P-256). Current seals also sign the time of sealing and which key was used. We then publish a certificate that anyone can check.

A seal shows
  • that a specific SecurityArts account sealed this exact file
  • when it was sealed
  • that the file hasn't changed by a single byte since
A seal doesn't show
  • who made the art, or whether a person or an AI made it
  • whether the work is original
  • who owns the copyright
  • anything about a copy that has been resized, re-saved, edited or screenshotted

A seal is a receipt, not a judgement, a certification of authorship, or a legal opinion. Don't describe it as more than that. The name a piece is "credited to" is whatever the sealing account typed in. We don't check it.

04Your work and your rights

  • You keep ownership of everything you upload.
  • You give us a worldwide, non-exclusive, royalty-free licence to store, copy, resize and show your work, its title and credit, and its certificate, for as long as it stays on SecurityArts. We use this only to run the service: the wall, certificates, your profile, the marketplace listings you choose, and checks that keep the service safe.
  • Sealed work is public. The image, title, credit, medium, the sealing account's name and handle, the fingerprint and the sealing time appear on the wall and on its certificate, and anyone can view them.

  • When you sell a piece, SecurityArts keeps 10% of the sale price and you keep 90%. On a direct commission, SecurityArts keeps 8% and you keep 92%. There are no listing fees.
  • Once payments are live, they are processed by our payment provider. Payouts go to your linked account within two business days of a completed sale. We never store full card numbers.
  • Work is licensed at one of three levels, shown at checkout: Personal (non-commercial display), Commercial (products, marketing or client work), or Exclusive (the buyer takes the work off sale). Sellers set their prices and confirm they're allowed to grant the licence they sell.
  • Any refund policy for a sale or commission is shown at checkout, before you pay.

You can delete work you sealed. It comes off the wall, and its certificate no longer resolves. If we receive a credible rights complaint, or a piece breaks these terms, we may take it down and mark or withdraw its certificate. Copyright complaints go to legal@securityarts.studio.
