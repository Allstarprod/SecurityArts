01 Pricing

Most of it is free.

Sealing your work and checking any seal cost nothing. Pro and Pro+ add extras for collectors and for artists who sell. Payments aren't live yet, so the paid plans can't be bought today.

02 Plans

Three plans. Yearly is two months free.

  • Free

    $0 forever

    For everyone: artists sealing their work, and anyone checking a seal.

    • Seal your finished work
    • Check any seal, no account needed
    • A public profile and your work on the wall
    • Browse and search the wall
    • Like the work you love
    Start free

  • Pro

    $5 a month, or $50 a year

    For collectors who want a sharper, deeper view of new work.

    • Everything in Free

    Planned, not built yet

    • Early access to newly sealed work
    • Saved searches
    • A provenance export of your collection
    Not on sale yet: start free

  • Pro+

    $15 a month, or $150 a year

    For artists who sell their work.

    • Everything in Free

    Planned, not built yet

    • Listings with licence tiers (Personal, Commercial, Exclusive)
    • Sales and audience analytics
    • Direct commissions
    • Payouts to your account
    Not on sale yet: start free

Paid plans open when payments go live. Until then, everyone is on Free.

03 Fees

What SecurityArts takes

No listing fees and no fees for sealing or checking. When selling opens, these are the only cuts.

10%
On marketplace sales. The artist keeps 90%.
8%
On direct commissions. The artist keeps 92%.
2 days
Payouts. Within two business days of a sale, once payments are live.

04 Questions

Questions

Can I buy Pro or Pro+ today?

Not yet. The prices are set, but payments aren't live, so nothing can be bought today. Everything in Free works now. We'll announce when paid plans open.

Is checking a seal extra?

No. Sealing your work and checking any seal are free on every plan, and checking never needs an account.

Will I pay to buy art?

No subscription is needed to collect. You'll pay the price of the work, and the 10% marketplace fee comes out of the sale. Buying opens when payments go live.

What does the artist keep?

90% of a marketplace sale and 92% of a direct commission. Once payments are live, payouts reach your linked account within two business days.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Plans will be month to month, or yearly with two months free. Cancel whenever; your certificates stay public either way.
