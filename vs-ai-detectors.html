Questions

  • Are AI detectors accurate?

    They estimate a probability from pixels and are frequently wrong in both directions — flagging human work as AI and missing AI as human. That false-positive problem is exactly why an artist needs something better than a detector to stand on.

  • Can I use a detector and a seal together?

    Yes. A detector can triage unknown images at scale; a seal proves you sealed a specific file at a recorded time. They answer different questions — one guesses about any image, the other is a verifiable receipt for yours.
