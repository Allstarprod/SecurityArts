01 Compare · AI detectors

Detectors guess. A seal records.

An AI detector looks at the pixels of any image and estimates how likely it is to be machine-made. A seal doesn't judge the pixels at all. It records which account sealed one exact file, and when, and anyone can check it.

02 Side by side

Two tools, two different questions

A detector asks how an image looks. A seal answers which account had this exact file, and when.

A SecurityArts seal and an AI detector, point by point

What it is A signed, public certificate for one file A machine-learning classifier
How it works Fingerprints the exact file bytes with SHA-256 and signs the fingerprint and the time Estimates a likelihood from the pixels

03 The fair take

Detectors have a real job

Detectors are useful for triaging unknown images at scale, like platform moderation, where nobody sealed anything and a quick estimate beats none.

Where they fall down is as evidence about one artist's piece. The score is a guess, and false positives are how real artists end up wrongly accused. Pointing to a detector that agrees with you invites the accuser to point to one that doesn't.

A seal doesn't answer the detector's question either. It can't show who made a piece, or how. What it adds is something a detector can't give you: a fixed record that your account had this exact file at a recorded time, and that it hasn't changed since.

Updated 10 October 2026. Tools change. If something here is out of date or unfair, tell us at hello@securityarts.studio.

04 Questions

Questions

Are AI detectors accurate?

They estimate a probability from the pixels, and they can be wrong in both directions: flagging human work as AI and missing AI work. Artists do get wrongly flagged, which is why a detector score is a weak thing to stand on in an argument about your own piece.

Does a seal prove my work isn't AI?

No. A seal records which account sealed an exact file, and when, and that the file hasn't changed since. It can't show whether a person or an AI made the work, and it never guesses.

Can I use a detector and a seal together?

Yes. A detector can triage unknown images at scale; a seal records which account sealed a specific file, and when. They answer different questions: one estimates something about any image, the other is a checkable record for a file that was sealed.
