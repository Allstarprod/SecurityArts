03 Method

How the engagement runs

  1. Scope to an outcome

    We agree what “good” looks like for your stage: a named goal, not a bundle of hours.

  2. Advise and coach

    We lead the decisions, run the workshops and build the program at the cadence you need.

05 The seal

The report is the deliverable

We seal the report with the studio's SecurityArts account. Anyone you share it with can check that it's the exact file we sealed, and when we sealed it.

What the seal shows

  • Which account sealed the report: the studio's.
  • When it was sealed, as recorded by SecurityArts.
  • That the file hasn't changed since. Change one character and it stops matching.

What it can't show

  • That your systems are secure. It covers the report, not the systems it describes.
  • Anything outside the scope we agreed, or anything found after the seal date.
  • An audit opinion or a certification.
  • A copy that has been edited, re-saved, re-exported or screenshotted. Only the exact file matches.

06 Questions

Questions

What does a vCISO engagement look like?

Fractional security leadership: strategy, risk decisions, roadmap, and the authority to say no when it matters, without the cost of a full-time hire. We meet at the cadence your stage needs.

Is this right for our stage?

It's aimed at startups and scaleups that have outgrown ad-hoc security but don't yet need a full-time CISO, often when a first enterprise deal or a SOC 2 push forces the question.

Is the engagement one-off or ongoing?

Either. A one-off workshop or program review, or an ongoing fractional retainer. Both end in a sealed report of the program's state and what to do next.
