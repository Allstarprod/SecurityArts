What's real

Rim, ring, field and star come from the mark's geometry (16 lobes on a notch circle, ring at 62%, star at 36%). About 256k triangles, one draw call. Die text and wax grain are a bump map.

Phones & reduced motion

Phones, reduced-motion users and browsers without WebGL get wax.svg on a slow CSS turntable. Next step: swap that for a frame sequence rendered from this scene.
