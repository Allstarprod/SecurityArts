How a seal works.

1

Upload the finished piece

Drop in the file you made. We read the exact bytes — nothing is altered, nothing is added on top.

2

We sign it

A SHA-256 fingerprint of those bytes, signed with our ECDSA key. Change one pixel and it no longer matches.

3

You get the receipt

A public certificate — hash, signature, timestamp. When someone says "that's AI," point at it instead of arguing.
