How the engagement runs

  1. Scope to an outcome. We agree on what "secure enough" means for this engagement — a named goal, not a fixed bundle of hours.
  2. Attack. Manual, adversary-style testing with the path written down as we go — reproducible, not a scanner dump.
  3. Seal. You get a signed, dated record: what was tested, what was proven safe, what is still open.
  4. Re-test. Fix the findings and we re-issue the seal — proof the fix actually held, not a one-day snapshot.
