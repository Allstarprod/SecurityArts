01 Compare · Not By AI

A statement vs a receipt.

The Not By AI badge is a label you add to your work to say a person made it. It raised real awareness, but it's self-declared: anyone can put the badge on any image, and there's nothing to check. A seal is a different kind of record: signed, dated, tied to the exact file, and checkable by anyone.

03 The fair take

Complementary, not rivals

The badge did something useful: it gave artists a way to plant a flag and pushed the conversation about AI-made work forward.

But a flag isn't evidence, and a seal doesn't settle the question either: it can't show that a person made the work. What it adds is a record a stranger can actually check: that your account had this exact file at a recorded time, and that it hasn't changed since.

Show the badge if it says what you stand for, and seal the piece so there's something to check.

04 Questions

Questions

Is the Not By AI badge useless?

No. It raised awareness and lets an artist signal intent, which has real value. Its limit is that it's self-declared: anyone can add the badge to any image, and there's nothing to check. It's a statement, not a record.

Can I use a badge and a seal together?

Yes. Show the badge to signal your stance, and seal the piece so there's something a stranger can actually check: which account sealed this exact file, and when.
