01 Legal

Privacy notice

Last updated 25 September 2026

Terms of Service

Draft for legal review. This version describes the data SecurityArts actually handles today. It still needs review by a lawyer, and the operating company (section 01) still needs to be filled in, before launch.

The short version

  • We collect what we need to run your account, seal your work and run the marketplace. We don't sell your data, and we don't use your work to train AI.
  • Sealed work is public by design: the image, its credit, the sealing account and the certificate.
  • Sign-up and sign-in are handled by Clerk. We never see your password.
  • You can see, correct, export or delete your data by emailing privacy@securityarts.studio.

SecurityArts is operated by [legal entity name and address, to be filled in], which decides how your data is used (the "controller"). Contact: privacy@securityarts.studio.

03Public by design

SecurityArts exists to publish certificates. When you seal a piece, its image, title, credit, medium, fingerprint and sealing time, and the sealing account's name and handle, appear on the wall and on its certificate page. Anyone can see them, and anyone can check a file against them. Your email address is never shown. If your profile is set to private, only your name and handle are shown on it.

05Cookies and browser storage

  • Sign-in cookies (set by Clerk, and our own session cookie) keep you signed in. They're essential, so they can't be switched off while you're signed in.
  • Analytics (PostHog) may store an identifier in your browser, but only where analytics is switched on. Where the law requires it, we ask first.
  • Small preferences, such as light or dark mode, are kept in your own browser.
  • No advertising cookies. No selling or sharing your data for ads.

06Your work and AI

We don't use your uploaded work, or anything else you give us, to train AI models, and we don't license it to anyone who does.

07How long we keep it

  • Account and profile data: while your account is open.
  • Sealed work: until you or we delete it. Once deleted, it comes off the wall and its certificate stops resolving.
  • Newsletter: until you unsubscribe.
  • Order and payout records: as long as tax and accounting law requires.
  • Security logs: a short rolling window, unless they're needed to investigate abuse.

08Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, the UK GDPR or the CCPA/CPRA), you can ask to see, correct, export or delete your data, restrict or object to how we use it, and withdraw consent at any time. Email privacy@securityarts.studio and we'll respond within the time the law sets. You can also complain to your local data-protection authority. When you delete your account, we remove it from Clerk and from our records and take your sealed work down. We can't recall copies other people have already saved.

09Security

Everything travels over HTTPS. Clerk handles passwords, so we never see them. Seals are signed with a key kept on our servers, and we use rate limiting, request checks, a strict Content-Security-Policy and audit logs. No system is perfectly secure; if something goes wrong that affects you, we'll tell you as the law requires.

10Children

SecurityArts isn't for anyone under 16, and we don't knowingly collect their data. If you think a child has signed up, email us and we'll delete the account.

11Changes

If we change how we handle data in a way that matters, we'll update this page with a new date and, where it affects you, tell you before it takes effect.
