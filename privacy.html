Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 July 2026 · Effective immediately

SecurityArts ("we", "us") builds tools that verify human-made art. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the rights you have. It covers the marketing site, the discovery app, and the marketplace. If you only browse, we collect very little; the sections below note exactly when more is collected.

Template notice. This document is a working draft written to reflect the product's actual data flows. Have it reviewed by qualified counsel before public launch, and localize for the jurisdictions you operate in.

1. What we collect

  • Newsletter email — only if you subscribe. Basis: your consent.
  • Account details — email, display name, and a password (stored only as a salted scrypt hash; we never see the plaintext). Basis: to provide the service.
  • Uploaded artwork & its seal — the image you submit for verification, plus the cryptographic certificate (content hash, signature, timestamp) we generate from it. Basis: to perform verification you request.
  • Order details — email, name, items, and (when payments launch) processing handled by our payment provider. We do not store full card numbers.
  • Technical & security data — IP address and request metadata, used for rate-limiting, fraud prevention, and audit logging. Basis: our legitimate interest in keeping the service secure.
  • Cookies — a single HttpOnly, SameSite session cookie when you sign in. No third-party advertising or tracking cookies.

2. How we use it

To operate the service (accounts, verification, boards, orders), to send the newsletter you opted into, to secure the platform against abuse, and to meet legal obligations. We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use it to train AI models.

3. Who we share it with

Only service providers needed to run the platform — e.g. a payment processor and an email delivery provider — under contract and only for those purposes. We may disclose information if required by law. We never sell or rent your data.

4. Your rights

Depending on where you live (GDPR / UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, PIPEDA and others), you may have the right to access, correct, delete, export, or restrict processing of your data, to withdraw consent, and to opt out of sale/sharing (we do neither). Exercise any of these by emailing privacy@securityarts.studio. We respond within the timeframes required by applicable law.

5. Retention

We keep data only as long as needed for the purpose collected: newsletter data until you unsubscribe; account and verification data while your account is active; order records as required for tax and accounting; security logs on a rolling short-term window.

6. Security

Data is transmitted over HTTPS. Passwords are salted and hashed with scrypt. Sessions use signed, HttpOnly cookies. We apply a Content-Security-Policy, security headers, rate limiting, same-origin request checks, and audit logging. No system is perfectly secure, but our controls are designed against the OWASP Top 10 and mapped to NIST CSF, ISO 27001, SOC 2, and CIS Benchmarks (see our security documentation).

7. Children

The service is not directed to children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

8. Changes & contact

We'll post any material changes here with a new effective date. Questions or requests: privacy@securityarts.studio.
