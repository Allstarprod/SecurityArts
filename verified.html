What the seal is — and isn't

What does the seal prove?

  • A specific file was sealed by your account.
  • It was sealed at a specific, recorded moment.
  • The exact bytes haven't changed since — and anyone can check that.

What doesn't the seal claim?

  • It's not an AI detector and never guesses.
  • It doesn't prove a human made the work, prove originality, or resolve copyright.
  • It doesn't judge the art — it only records who sealed what, and when.
