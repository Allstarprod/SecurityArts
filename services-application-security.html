03 Method

How the engagement runs

  1. Scope to an outcome

    We agree what “secure enough” means for this surface: a named goal, not a bundle of hours.

  2. Model and review

    Threat-model first, then read the code by hand, with the path written down as we go.

  3. Seal the report

    A signed, dated report: what we reviewed, what held up, and what is still open. Sealed, so anyone you share it with can check it hasn't changed.

  4. Retest

    Fix the findings and we test them again. The retest gets its own sealed report, so you can show the fix was checked, and when.

05 The seal

The report is the deliverable

We seal the report with the studio's SecurityArts account. Anyone you share it with can check that it's the exact file we sealed, and when we sealed it.

What the seal shows

  • Which account sealed the report: the studio's.
  • When it was sealed, as recorded by SecurityArts.
  • That the file hasn't changed since. Change one character and it stops matching.

What it can't show

  • That your systems are secure. It covers the report, not the systems it describes.
  • Anything outside the scope we agreed, or anything found after the seal date.
  • An audit opinion or a certification.
  • A copy that has been edited, re-saved, re-exported or screenshotted. Only the exact file matches.

06 Questions

Questions

How is this different from running a SAST scanner?

Scanners flag patterns; we review logic. A person reads the auth flows, trust boundaries and business logic a scanner can't reason about, and every finding comes with a reproducible path, not a wall of low-signal alerts.

Do you review the whole codebase or a slice?

We scope to the highest-risk surface first: auth, payments, data access and anything internet-facing. Then we widen as budget allows. The sealed report records exactly what was reviewed and what wasn't.

Will our engineers actually keep the secure SDLC?

That's the point. We build guardrails into the flow your team already uses rather than handing over a policy PDF, and we coach the team so the next feature starts secure.
