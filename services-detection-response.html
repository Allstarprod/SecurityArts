How the engagement runs

  1. Scope to an outcome. Agree the techniques and assets that matter most — a named goal, not a bundle of hours.
  2. Emulate & engineer. Run the attacks, measure coverage, build and tune the detections that were missing.
  3. Seal. A signed, dated record: what you can detect now, and where the gaps still are.
  4. Re-test. Re-run the emulation and re-issue the seal — proof the coverage held.
