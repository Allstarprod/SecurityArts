02 The Seal

Every engagement
ends in a mark.

The seal is the whole idea. A notary's stamp says a person stood here and verified this. Ours says the same about your systems. When we close an engagement, you don't get a wall of findings — you get a record of exactly what was tested, what was proven safe, and what is still open, signed and dated.

  • Verifiable Every claim traces to a test you can re-run.
  • Honest We mark what's still open as plainly as what's fixed.
  • Durable A retest re-issues the seal — it isn't a one-day snapshot.

03 Identity

One logo. Any surface.

The mark is contrast-aware. A single-ink master drops onto anything — from a billboard to a 16-pixel favicon — and takes black or white depending on what the surface needs. No color version required to be recognizable.
